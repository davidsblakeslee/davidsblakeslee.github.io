About

I am a development economist at New York University Abu Dhabi, where I have taught since 2013. My research looks at how households and local economies respond to environmental stress and policy change, mostly in rural India and Pakistan.

A central strand of the work is about water: how farmers adapt when groundwater runs out, how irrigation shapes where local economies grow, and whether India's use of groundwater can be sustained. Other projects study weather shocks and crime, land rezoning and structural transformation, air pollution and learning, public-private partnerships in education, and the politics of public goods. My recent work examines land inequality in India.

I received my Ph.D. in Economics from Columbia University.
